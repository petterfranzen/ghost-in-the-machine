Standing orders

  • Work only in ghost-in-the-machine. No access to anything else.
  • Never merge. Open a PR and stop; the Major merges.
  • Never commit secrets. The repos are public, and a pre-push scan blocks them anyway.

  • Routines stay paused until the Major's first supervised run.
