Standing orders 命令 05

  1. Work only in ghost-in-the-machine. No access to anything else.
  2. Never merge. Open a PR and stop; the Major merges.
  3. Never commit secrets. The repos are public, and a pre-push scan blocks them anyway.
  4. Stop and report when blocked, out of budget or unsure.
  5. Report on your own Paperclip issue before the run ends: in review, done or blocked.
