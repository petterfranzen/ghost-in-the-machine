01

01 — Why

Two rooms, one set of parts.

The ops room and the client-facing room should not be two design languages that happen to share a repo.

02

The problem

Every surface was solving the same problems alone

  • Three palettes, four type stacks and no shared spacing scale.
  • Tables, cards and stat tiles rebuilt per page, each with its own contrast mistakes.
  • Decks drifting from the dashboard they described.
  • No machine-readable tokens, so slides and scripts guessed.

One base fixes all four without flattening the two looks into one.

03

The answer

One base, two themes

Professional takes its tokens from the approved portfolio: Inter, one accent, soft surfaces, dark and light.

Section 9 is the GITS-95 ops room: mono body, Rajdhani display, phosphor green, zero radius, amber focus. Dark only, on purpose.
